Our roots

Neighbors, veterans and local partners working together

A Better Tomorrow World is a Florida nonprofit corporation, incorporated October 1, 2026, and built as a public-private model. Our volunteers, veteran mentors and private-sector partners provide the labor and funding, so public resources go further.

We start with one building in Intercession City. Once it is fully restored inside and out, it becomes a shared home for direct-support services. As our community grows stronger, we will open a larger regional site for trade training and affordable childcare.

Our approach

We are the last mile

Central Florida already has strong nonprofits and agencies doing important work. We don’t duplicate what others do well. We serve as their local hub and point of contact, bringing their food, services and programs the last mile to families in Intercession City and Campbell City who are hard to reach.

Local familiesFood, benefits enrollment, tax help and a path to owning a home.

YouthAfter-school tutoring, character education, a hot meal and hands-on gardening.

Returning citizensService crews, benefits navigation and trade training that lead to steady work.

Student veteransTuition and fee coverage in exchange for mentoring local kids each week.

The trunk

The Community Empowerment & Re-entry Hub

The county-owned modular building at 1525 Immokalee Street has sat vacant and overgrown. We have asked Osceola County to lease it to us for community use under Florida Statute § 125.38, which lets counties lease surplus property to nonprofits for a nominal fee.

Our lead corporate partner, TPKBPRO Commercial Kitchen Installation, has committed its licensed crews to repair, paint, pressure-wash and landscape the building at no cost to the county. Programs open only after the building is fully restored.

Phase 1 programs

What the hub will offer

Everything below launches once the building is restored. Where another organization already does the work well, we don’t compete with it. We bring its services the last mile, right to our neighbors.

Youth · Veteran-led

Vanguard Scholars After-School Program

We cover tuition and fees for student veterans at Valencia College and Osceola Technical College (oTECH), filling gaps the Post-9/11 GI Bill leaves or helping veterans whose benefits have run out. In return, each Vanguard Mentor gives 7 to 10 hours a week tutoring kids at the hub.

  • Daily reading, writing and math tutoring
  • Travis Manion Foundation “Character Does Matter” lessons, taught face to face with no screens
  • Every child eats a nutritious hot meal before going home

Families

SNAP & healthcare navigation

Staffed computer stations where trained volunteers help families and returning citizens apply for SNAP, Medicaid and county health programs, without needing a ride across the county.

Food security

Food & crisis outreach

With Second Harvest Food Bank of Central Florida and Living Water Fellowship Food Pantry, the hub serves as a local storage and pickup point for emergency food bags and nutrition boxes, closer to the families who need them.

Neighborhood · Veteran-led

Operation Legacy service crews

Veterans from AMVETS Intercession City lead youth, young adults and returning citizens in cleaning up blight, painting seniors’ homes and pressure-washing properties. Crews also join seasonal cleanup days and honor fallen heroes at local cemeteries around Memorial Day.

If not me, then who…

Families

Homeownership counseling

NCHEC-certified counselors teach credit-building, budgeting and home-buying, step by step, so renters can become owners and build wealth that stays in the family.

Youth · Environment

Youth horticulture & science

The land around the building becomes community gardens. Kids learn plant biology and environmental science while growing fresh produce for local families.

Seniors · Food security

Senior food delivery: the last mile

We aim to serve as the local last-mile partner for established senior meal and food programs. Our hub receives the food, and our volunteer drivers bring it to homebound seniors in Intercession City and Campbell City, so no neighbor is missed.

The branches · Phase 2

Building paths to good-paying work

As the hub’s families gain stability and our funding grows, we will open a larger site in the region, built with CareerSource Central Florida and the Osceola Chamber of Commerce. Phase 2 turns today’s volunteer programs into paid jobs and training for returning citizens, at-risk youth and their families.

Adult upskilling & trade academy

Intensive, multi-week certification courses for adults, at-risk youth and returning citizens, connected directly to high-demand jobs with employers across Osceola and Orange counties. TPKBPRO will give our graduates first consideration for paid apprenticeships.

Commercial kitchen installationWeldingRefrigeration repairHVAC

Affordable community daycare

Parents can’t work or attend class without childcare they can afford. An on-site daycare removes that barrier so single parents and low-income families can train for, and keep, career-level jobs.

Paid work crews

Our volunteer service crews grow into paid crews for landscaping, pressure-washing and property upkeep for local businesses, churches and property owners. Returning citizens and young adults earn a paycheck, trade experience and a reference that helps them land a higher-wage job.

Community kitchen & culinary training

A licensed commercial kitchen that cooks meals for our youth and senior programs while training residents in food safety and food service, leading to certification and catering and restaurant jobs.

How our paid positions work

Our paid roles are stepping stones, not permanent jobs. Each one gives a person real work experience and a strong reference so they can move up to a higher-wage job, which opens the spot for the next person.

  1. TrainEarn certifications through the trade academy and workforce training.
  2. WorkTake a paid position on a crew, in the kitchen or on delivery routes and build a resume.
  3. Move upStep into a higher-wage job with an employer partner, with our references behind you.
  4. Open the doorYour spot goes to the next applicant, and the cycle starts again.
